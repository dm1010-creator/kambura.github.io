Understanding Fire & Damp

Ayurveda

Agni represents digestive and metabolic fire. Traditional Ayurvedic practice views health as maintaining balanced Agni rather than extinguishing it. When fire becomes excessively sharp (Tikshna Agni), cooling foods, calming routines, and restorative herbs are traditionally explored.

Traditional Chinese Medicine

Damp Heat is a traditional TCM pattern describing warmth combined with heaviness or stagnation. Traditional approaches include cooling acupressure points, herbal wisdom, and lifestyle practices intended to encourage harmony.
